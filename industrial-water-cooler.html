Engineering Quality

Built For Tough Industrial Environments

Engineered to deliver clean, chilled drinking water under continuous high-duty shifts.

🛡️

Heavy-Gauge Stainless Steel Body

Crafted with corrosion-resistant stainless steel sheet metal that withstands humid, oily, and corrosive factory atmospheres without rust.

🪣

Hygienic SS304 Internal Tank

Fully welded food-grade SS304 water tank prevents bacterial buildup, scaling, and water discoloration, ensuring 100% safe drinking water.

❄️

High-Efficiency Cooling Coils

100% electrolytic copper coils wrapped seamlessly around the stainless steel tank provide rapid thermal transfer and fast chilling recovery.

🌡️

Dense PUF Thermal Insulation

Injected polyurethane foam insulation locks in cold water temperature for hours, significantly cutting down compressor cycling and power bills.

⚙️

Tropicalized Hermetic Compressor

Heavy-duty compressors rated for ambient temperatures up to 48°C, equipped with internal thermal overload protectors.

🚰

Solid Brass Chrome-Plated Faucets

Durable, push-type or lever faucets designed for high-frequency daily usage without dripping, leaking, or thread wear.
