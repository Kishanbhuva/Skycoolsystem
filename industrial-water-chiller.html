Cooling Configurations

Engineered For Every Industrial Process

Selecting the optimal cooling system based on your facility layout and water availability.

💨

Air-Cooled Industrial Chillers

Self-contained, plug-and-play design rejecting heat directly into ambient air using high-efficiency axial fans and copper-finned condenser coils. Ideal for facilities without access to an external cooling tower.

  • ✓ No external cooling tower or makeup water required
  • ✓ Heavy-gauge powder coated casing for harsh shop floors
  • ✓ Low maintenance axial fan assemblies
  • ✓ Compact footprint with integrated circulation pump
🌊

Water-Cooled Industrial Chillers

Connected to a cooling tower for continuous high-COP thermal heat rejection. Designed for higher tonnages, uncompromised continuous duty in enclosed indoor plant rooms with superior energy efficiency.

  • ✓ High COP efficiency in high-ambient tropical environments
  • ✓ Shell and tube water-cooled condenser with cleanable tubes
  • ✓ Silent indoor operation
  • ✓ Ideal for heavy manufacturing plants and central cooling

Stainless Steel Insulated Water Tank

Food-grade stainless steel (SS304) reservoir tank insulated with dense PUF prevents corrosion, water contamination, and heat loss.

Microcontroller Digital Interface

Precision digital temperature controller displaying actual process fluid temperature and setpoint with intuitive adjustment (0.1°C resolution).

🔒

Comprehensive Safety Interlocks

Equipped with High/Low refrigerant pressure switches, anti-freeze thermostat, water flow switch, motor thermal overload, and phase reverse protection.

Integrated multi-stage centrifugal pump guarantees steady flow and pressure through mould cooling channels, laser optics, or reactors.
