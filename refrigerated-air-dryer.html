Design Features

Why Sky Cool Air Dryers Outperform

Precision engineering built into every unit at our Ahmedabad manufacturing works.

🌀

Copper "Tube & Tube" & "Shell & Tube" Core

100% non-corrosive copper heat exchanger designed for minimal pressure drop and maximum thermal transfer with an in-built centrifugal moisture separator having a dedicated dead zone to prevent condensate carryover.

🛡️

PUF Insulated Exchanger

High-density injected Polyurethane Foam (PUF) insulation around the heat exchanger assembly stops cold loss and prevents external sweating.

⏱️

Electronic Digital Auto Drain Valve

Equipped with fully adjustable interval and discharge time digital timer auto drain valves to purge accumulated condensate reliably.

🔄

Non-Cyclic Hot Gas Bypass Solenoid

Maintains stable evaporating temperature and prevents evaporator freezing under variable air demand loads and seasonal changes.

⚙️

Standardized Global Components

Uses universally serviceable hermetic/scroll compressors, high-efficiency condenser fan motors, filters, and expansion valves.

🌱

Ozone-Friendly Refrigerants

Charged with zero ozone-depleting refrigerants including R134a, R22, R410A, and R407C ensuring environmental compliance.
