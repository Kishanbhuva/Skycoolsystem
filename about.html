Quality Assurance

Tested & Verified Manufacturing Standards

Strict quality control across raw material inspection, pressure testing, refrigeration cycle verification, and final electrical run tests.

🧪

Pressure & Leak Testing

Every copper heat exchanger and refrigeration circuit is hydrostatic and nitrogen pressure tested up to 25+ bar to guarantee zero leakage in demanding shop-floor conditions.

📊

Dew Point Performance Validation

Refrigerated air dryers are rigorously tested under simulated inlet temperature loads to ensure constant +3°C pressure dew point stability across 20 to 500 CFM ratings.

⚡

Electrical Safety & Automation

Equipped with branded electrical switchgear, phase sequence protection, digital temperature displays, and electronic timer auto-drain systems.
